Our little story

Read this one without trying to find the next button immediately.

Devi,

Some memories become special because something enormous happened. Other memories become special for absolutely no obvious reason. A random picture. A ridiculous joke. A normal afternoon. A look that lasted for half a second.

This website is made from those little things. Not one giant event. Just dozens of tiny moments that somehow became important enough to keep.

I hope this birthday gives you more reasons to smile, more things to look forward to, more places to see, more people who make you laugh, and more days that feel worth remembering.

And somewhere between the serious plans and completely random chaos, I hope there are many more photographs waiting to happen.

Also, after seeing how many photos made it into this website, I think the camera roll deserves a small apology.

Happy Birthday, Devi. ♡
